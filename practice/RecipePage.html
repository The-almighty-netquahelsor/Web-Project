Ingredients

  • 4 Raw Eggs
  • 1½ Cup of Groundnut oil
  • Salt
  • Fresh Pepper
  • Onion
  • Fresh Tomatoes
  • 2 Seasoned Cube Star of Maggi
  • Seasoned Ginger or Industrial Ginger
  • Nutmeg

Instructions

  1. Put your pan on fire
  2. Break your eggs into a bowl
  3. Stir it until it's well mixed
  4. Add your Groundnut oil inside the heated pan after 3 minutes on fire
  5. Blend your Onion, fresh Pepper and the fresh Tomatoes
  6. stim the blended items and let dry
  7. Now add stimmed items into the heated pan
  8. stir for 5 minutes
  9. Then add your seasoning;
    • A Tip Spoon of Salt
    • The 2 maggi Cube
    • Seasoned Ginger
    • Nutmeg
  10. Stir until cooked well for 5 minutes
